## Names retained without a logo

Broadway; Gelato Maestro; New Cathay; Ecom; Alipore Bungalow; Cafe Marrakech; Amber; Foi; Utsav; Fairweather Banquets; Playport Sports; Shoreline Cafe; Skipper Online; Renoirr; Hardware.

These names were researched with Kolkata as the location. No logo has been substituted where the business identity or artwork could not be verified. Some businesses have no reachable official site, an expired/parked domain, or multiple unrelated namesakes.

Notable exclusions:
- gelato-maestro.com is a gelato-formulation software app, not the Southern Avenue ice-cream business.
- skipperonline.com redirects to an unrelated education business; skipperfurnishings.com confirms the furnishings business but does not supply a separate verified Skipper Online mark.
- bsirkarjohuree.com currently serves a parked Squarespace page.
- thealiporebungalow.com redirects to a parked landing page.
- revercafe.com currently shows a generic launching-soon page without an identifiable Kolkata brand mark.
- The Park Kolkata confirms Someplace Else and Aqua, but a parent hotel logo has not been substituted for either venue.
- Generic names such as Ecom, Golden, Choice, Ganpati, Foi, Utsav and Hardware have multiple possible matches; a matching name alone is insufficient.

To add a confirmed mark, save the asset in this folder and update verifiedLogos in lib/brands.ts. All 40 supplied names remain in the carousel.
